# Research Problem Statement

## Project Title
Developing and Validating a Cost-Effective Network Segmentation and Cyber Resilience Framework for Small U.S. Manufacturing Businesses

## Background
Small manufacturing businesses depend on computer networks to support office operations, production-related activities, and communication between systems. Some may face challenges in implementing cybersecurity safeguards because of limited budgets, staffing, or specialized expertise.

## Research Problem
Network segmentation can help restrict unnecessary communication between different systems. However, smaller manufacturers may need practical assistance translating existing cybersecurity guidance into network configurations, implementation plans, cost estimates, and repeatable validation tests. Whether and to what extent these gaps exist will be examined through further research.

## Proposed Research
This project will investigate a cost-conscious network segmentation methodology using Cisco Packet Tracer simulations, cybersecurity risk assessment, documented test cases, and implementation planning tools.

## Research Objectives
1. Review relevant NIST and CISA guidance.
2. Design a simulated small manufacturing business network.
3. Compare baseline and segmented network configurations.
4. Document permitted and restricted network communication.
5. Develop preliminary assessment and implementation checklists.
6. Seek qualified technical feedback and refine the framework.

## Scope and Limitations
The initial work uses simulated networks and does not involve real manufacturing equipment or operational technology. The project does not claim proven cybersecurity improvements in actual businesses. Any future real-world evaluation would require appropriate authorization and safety controls.

## Current Status
Initial research and project setup. Experimental results and external validation are pending.
